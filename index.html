Questions

How far ahead should I order?

At least a day ahead, so we can bake it fresh for you. If something is marked "In stock today", you can order it for today.

How much is delivery?

It depends on your area. We confirm the delivery fee on WhatsApp before we bake.

How do I pay?

You choose how to pay when you order, and we confirm it on WhatsApp.

Can I choose the chocolate?

Yes. Every cookie and cookie box comes in white, milk or dark chocolate.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes, for parties, offices and holidays. Send us the date and the quantity on WhatsApp.
